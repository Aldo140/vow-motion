# Outreach market priority

## Recommendation

New Jersey is the first state to target for Vow Motion. The state combines the highest reported average wedding cost, high household income, dense access to New York clients, estate and shore venues, and the multiday guest logistics that make household invitations, event access, meals, travel details and updates valuable.

Massachusetts and Connecticut are the next Northeast markets. California and Florida are the strongest destination market complements because their vineyard, coastal and resort weddings create more guest travel and multievent complexity.

## Evidence

- CNBC reports that New Jersey has the highest average wedding cost at $54,400: https://www.cnbc.com/select/average-cost-of-a-wedding/
- The Wedding Report lists 2025 average costs of $49,281 in Massachusetts and $46,794 in New Jersey: https://wedding.report/action/wedding_statistics/view/2025_average_wedding_cost_by_state/
- Federal Reserve Economic Data reports 2024 median household incomes of $103,500 in New Jersey, $113,900 in Massachusetts and $99,240 in Connecticut: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/release/tables?eid=259462&rid=249
- The CDC publishes marriage counts and rates by state for weighting market volume: https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/state-stats/more-maps/marriage.html
- The Knot's 2025 study reports a $34,000 national average wedding cost and $24,000 median: https://s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/theknot.com/xo-content/pdf/the-knot-real-weddings-study-data-read-out-2026.pdf

## Target order

1. New Jersey
2. Massachusetts
3. Connecticut
4. New York suburbs and the Hudson Valley
5. Maryland and Washington DC
6. California wine country and coastal destination markets
7. Florida luxury and destination markets

The practical outreach unit is a planner serving complex weddings, rather than every wedding business in the state. Prefer full service planning, destination work, wedding weekends, multicultural celebrations and firms that emphasize guest experience.
