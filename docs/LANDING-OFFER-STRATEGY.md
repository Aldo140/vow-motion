# Vow Motion offer audit

Reviewed September 8, 2026. This is an application of Hormozi's publicly available offer and acquisition materials, not a claim to have read every book in full. Recommendations below are hypotheses, not measured conversion improvements.

## Diagnosis

The existing visual experience demonstrates care and taste. The copy leaves too much of the product and offer implicit. A visitor sees romance before understanding what they can do, and the planner page frames adoption as running a real wedding before establishing whether the product is useful. Cathy's response is direct evidence of switching resistance from one prospect, not proof of a market wide preference.

The strongest immediate offer is an interactive sample that answers a narrow question: would this invitation experience suit my wedding or clients? It already exists, works without signup, and requires neither a meeting nor real guest data. Put it before account creation for visitors who are still evaluating.

## Principles and application

| Principle | Interpretation for this product | Implementation or boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Desired outcome | Couples want a personal first impression and organized guest information. Planners want their design to carry into the guest experience. | Make invitations, household RSVPs, events and travel explicit in the hero. Preserve the visual identity that demonstrates the outcome. |
| Belief that it will work | A small team has limited customer proof. A working experience provides inspectable product evidence. | Lead with an actual guest demo and label sample data. Do not substitute fictional customer counts, testimonials or results. |
| Time to value | A visitor can evaluate the opening and RSVP immediately. | Guest preview first, with Studio as the next evaluation step. No invented setup time promise. |
| Effort and sacrifice | Creating an account, booking a meeting, importing guests and switching systems are separate costs. | No signup for the initial demo. Explain the three steps and keep real guest data out of evaluation. |
| Offer construction | Group capabilities around the job they perform rather than making a long feature claim. | Connect invitation, replies, guest details and Studio. State current release pricing prominently. |
| Lead generation | A useful sample can earn interest before a larger ask. | Retain ungated guest and Studio demos. Account creation follows demonstrated value. An email gate would add friction to the current outreach audience. |
| Objection handling | Existing software is a real constraint. A prettier page cannot eliminate duplicate work. | The planner page states coexistence in its hero and again in an "alongside, not instead" band, and leads with the supplier exports rather than the guest list. The FAQ states plainly that there is no live two-way sync and that movement is by CSV in both directions. No competitor is named on the page: naming one invites the comparison rather than dissolving it. |
| Risk reduction | Evaluation can be bounded without making commercial guarantees. | Sample weddings, no card, no real guest contact, preview before sharing. No refund promise or guaranteed business result. |
| Offer naming | The next action should explain what the user receives. | Concrete guest invitation and Studio labels. Keep the established Vow Motion name. |
| Bonuses | Extras should remove a specific obstacle and be deliverable. | Explain existing setup guidance and import support. Do not attach invented dollar values to included features. |
| Scarcity and urgency | Capacity or deadlines must be real and maintained. | Remove vague limited planner recruitment language. No countdown, fake availability counter or deadline. Revisit only with actual staffing and cohort dates. |
| Monetization | Price and packaging affect delivery obligations as well as conversion. | Clarify the existing no charge current release offer. Do not invent a subscription, lifetime deal, discount anchor or future pricing policy. |

## Page sequence

Home: tangible outcome and working preview, emotional context, design collection, three setup steps, connected capabilities, planner path, explicit current offer, objections, creation CTA.

Planners: client outcome and guest preview, evaluation steps, Studio to guest feature pairs, private demo offer, existing workflow and pricing answers, Studio CTA.

The complimentary personalized concept offered to Cathy remains an individual outreach offer. Public pages do not promise unlimited bespoke work or a turnaround time the team has not established.

## Sources and limits

- [Official Offers material](https://www.acquisition.com/offers-oo): value equation, offer stacking, objection handling and risk reduction.
- [Official book description](https://shop.acquisition.com/products/100m-offers-hardcover): value exchange, pricing and offer construction.
- [Official Leads resources](https://www.acquisition.com/leads-gifts): lead generation system and ungated access to resources.
- [Author's bonus chapters](https://www.acquisition.com/hubfs/2%20Bonus%20Chapters.pdf): early customer acquisition with free or discounted entry offers; the offer must still deliver value. This supports testing an accessible entry experience, not making all future service free.
- [Guarantee checklist](https://www.acquisition.com/hubfs/Offer%20Checklists%20-%20PDF%20Downloads/Unbeatable-Guarantee-Checklist.pdf): several ways to allocate risk. We apply bounded evaluation rather than financial guarantees.
- [Scarcity and urgency checklist](https://www.acquisition.com/hubfs/Offer%20Checklists%20-%20PDF%20Downloads/Scarcity-Urgency-Checklist.pdf): capacity and time based mechanisms. No operational basis currently exists for a new public scarcity claim.

## Evaluation after release

Assess the full path: qualified visits to demo openings, successful invitation interactions, Studio exploration, account creation and completed wedding setup. More demo clicks alone are not evidence of more customers. Separate planner outreach traffic from couple traffic where consented measurement is available. Ask planners whether the demo clarified both value and required work. Collect permission before publishing any feedback as a testimonial.

This change adds no tracking, email sequence or automatic outreach. Existing interaction, keyboard navigation and reduced motion behavior must continue to work. Validate desktop and narrow phone layouts, FAQ keyboard operation, demo redirects and setup navigation before release.
