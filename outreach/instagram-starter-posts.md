# Instagram starter posts

Six posts to open the account. Paste each into `/admin/content` → **New post**,
attach the media noted, set a publish time. Suggested cadence: one every 3–4 days
so the first fortnight is covered.

Tone: first person, honest that it is pre-launch, no invented traction. Matches
[../docs/PLANNER-OUTREACH.md](../docs/PLANNER-OUTREACH.md) — nothing here claims a
planner uses it or a wedding has run through it.

---

## 1 — Introduction (single image)

**Media:** a clean shot of the invitation on a phone (the `riviera` demo opened
on a device), or the wordmark on the off-white/olive brand background.

> I build Vow Motion.
>
> It is the guest-facing side of a wedding — the private invitation each
> household opens, their RSVP, their travel and seating — built around the way a
> planner actually runs a weekend rather than around a wedding website.
>
> Still early. No planner uses it yet and no wedding has run through it. I am
> looking for the first few. If you plan weddings, or you are planning your own,
> follow along — I will be showing the real thing as it gets built.
>
> —
> vowmotion.ca

---

## 2 — The core idea (single image or short video)

**Media:** screen-recording or screenshot of a household invitation — the
envelope opening to a page addressed to "The Rossi household", showing only
their events.

> One invitation per household. Not a link everyone shares.
>
> Each household opens a page addressed to them by name — no account, no
> password — and sees only the events they are invited to. The welcome dinner
> stays invisible to the people who were not asked to it.
>
> Their RSVP comes back per person and per event, with the meal and any dietary
> note attached. You are not chasing a spreadsheet.

## 4 — The output angle (single image)

**Media:** a screenshot of the kitchen sheet or seating export — the CSV/table
of covers by meal with dietary notes.

> The documents you rebuild by hand the week before — generated from the
> replies as they land.
>
> - kitchen sheet: covers by meal, every dietary note listed
> - shuttle manifest, from your own transport question
> - place cards with guest, table, meal and dietary
>

## Notes

- **Hashtags:** put 3–5 on their own line at the very end, specific not broad —
  `#weddingplanner #weddingplanning #calgaryweddings #weddingtech` beats
  `#wedding #love #bride`.
- **Link in bio:** point it at `vowmotion.ca` (or `vow-motion.vercel.app` until
  the domain is live) — the `/planners` page for the planner posts, `/demo/riviera`
  for the couple-facing ones. Swap it when a post references it.
- **First image:** post 1 or 2 first; a brand-new account posting a 6-slide
  carousel as its opening move reads as spam to the ranking system.
