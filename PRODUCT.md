# Vow Motion

## Register
product

## Users
Couples planning at a laptop in daylight; planners managing several weddings; guests opening a personal invitation on a phone. Guest access requires no account.

## Product Purpose
One authoritative guest list connects invitations, private events, RSVP, travel, seating, and memories. SaaS underneath; bespoke creative experience on top.

## Brand Personality
Understated, intimate, confident. A restrained gallery surrounding expressive weddings. User-approved direction is recorded in docs/MASTER-BRIEF.md.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS card grids, gradient blobs, romantic clichés, fake social proof, unrelated stacked website sections, freeform design tools.

## Design Principles
- Guest logistics must share one source of truth.
- Access control belongs on the server.
- Curated art direction protects the result.
- The invitation, RSVP, and wedding pass belong to one world.
- Useful mobile information is always reachable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive layouts, English/Spanish guest UI. No forced audio.
